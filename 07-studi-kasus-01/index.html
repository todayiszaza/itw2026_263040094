About WPU StreamFest

WPU StreamFest 2026 is a three-day music festival that brings together six popular K-pop groups for an unforgettable musical celebration. Inspired by the theme "Neon Nights", the festival offers a vibrant atmosphere filled with colorful lights, energetic performances, and exciting experiences for music lovers. Visitors can enjoy different musical performances while exploring various entertainment areas throughout the event.

Designed especially for Gen Z and young music enthusiasts, WPU StreamFest is more than just a music festival. It also promotes an eco-friendly environment through waste management and sustainable practices. The festival provides cashless payment systems, interactive activities, food and beverage areas, and comfortable spaces where visitors can relax and connect with others. Our goal is to create a memorable experience that combines music, creativity, and sustainability.
