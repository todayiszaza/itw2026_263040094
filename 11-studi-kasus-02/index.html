About NewJeans

NewJeans is a South Korean girl group known for their refreshing musical style and unique visual identity. The group officially debuted in July 2022 under ADOR.

Their music combines elements of K-Pop, R&B, and dance pop. NewJeans is recognized for its nostalgic aesthetics, catchy melodies, and youthful concepts.
